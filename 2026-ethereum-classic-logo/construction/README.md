# Construction drawings

The geometry and the facets of the Ethereum Classic Logo, drawn from the same measured master as the SVG and PNG
files. u is one thousandth of the logo's height; x is measured from the centre line, y down from the top tip.

| File | Size | Kind | Bytes |
|---|---|---|---|
| [ethereum-classic-logo-construction.svg](ethereum-classic-logo-construction.svg) | 1960 x 1380 | vector | 15456 |
| [ethereum-classic-logo-construction.png](ethereum-classic-logo-construction.png) | 1960 x 1380 | RGB, white | 187323 |
| [ethereum-classic-logo-facets.svg](ethereum-classic-logo-facets.svg) | 1800 x 1180 | vector | 7738 |
| [ethereum-classic-logo-facets.png](ethereum-classic-logo-facets.png) | 1800 x 1180 | RGB, white | 133866 |

- `ethereum-classic-logo-construction`: the outline, the mirror axes, the facet split, the gaps and gap bands, both tip angles,
  height and width, and every outline point with its value.
- `ethereum-classic-logo-facets`: the eight facets named, with their Full colour and Mono values.

The SVGs use the DejaVu Sans font with generic fall-backs; the PNGs were rendered with DejaVu Sans, so they look
the same everywhere. Specification and colours in full: [../README.md](../README.md#specification).
