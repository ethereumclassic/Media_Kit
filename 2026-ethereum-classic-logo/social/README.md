# Social media

Profile pictures and icons of the Ethereum Classic logo (rebuilt, not official files) for 8 platforms,
one folder per platform. Sizes follow each platform's published specs (checked 2026-10-20); values the
platforms do not publish are marked "not published" in the platform's README, with the size this set uses and why.

Backgrounds: Black `#000000` and White `#ffffff` only. Variants: Full colour on White, Full colour on Black, Mono on
Black. Icons shown without a background (watermark, emoji, stickers, role icons) are transparent.

Circle crop: profile pictures are square, and the logo is 67 % of the height and centred, as on the @eth_classic
profile picture (measured: 267.1 of 400 px). Its tips are 0.335 of the side from the centre, inside the circle
the platforms show (radius 0.5).
