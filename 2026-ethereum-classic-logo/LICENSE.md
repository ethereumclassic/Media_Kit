## Changes

The logo was rebuilt as a vector from values measured on the logo images Ethereum Classic publishes (the
2016 Media Kit PNG, the 2025 brand guide, the 2025 guide's download, the @eth_classic profile picture and
the CoinGecko icon). The outline was made mirror-symmetric left to right and top to bottom, because every
official image agrees with that to within one of its own pixels. The PNG files are rendered from the
vector.
